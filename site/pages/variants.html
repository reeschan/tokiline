Variants and Status

Variants give an Item its weight: a compact Item keeps only its text, while a milestone makes its Marker larger and its Card border the accent color. Status shows where an Item stands: done keeps the default look, current adds a ring to its Marker, and upcoming draws its Segment dashed.
