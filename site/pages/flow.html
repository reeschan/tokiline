Flow

A Flow lays its Items out along several Lanes. The Lane Columns form a narrow band whose width is the number of Lanes times --toki-lane-gap, and every Card and Meta lines up in one column outside that band. The attributes below are the output of flowAttributes(), written by hand as a plain HTML author would.
