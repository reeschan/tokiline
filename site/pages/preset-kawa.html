Preset: kawa

kawa draws progress like a river. An Item with Status done gets a solid accent Segment, an upcoming one a dashed Segment whose pattern drifts toward the end of the Line, and where scroll-driven animations are supported the done Segments fill as they are read. Scroll down to watch the line grow.
