Preset in a Flow

A Flow draws the Lane Lines and the Fork and Join bends through the same pseudo-elements that the Presets use for their motion. Each fixture below is the same Flow, with the same six Items on two Lanes, once with no Preset and once with each Preset. The first fixture keeps the default look; the others must keep the Flow's Lines and bends whole. The attributes are the output of flowAttributes(), written by hand as a plain HTML author would.
